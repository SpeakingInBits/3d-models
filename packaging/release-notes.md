## Installing

Download [`ModelConfigurator-win-x64-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/SpeakingInBits/3d-models/releases/download/v{{VERSION}}/ModelConfigurator-win-x64-Setup.exe) and double-click it. It's for 64-bit Windows on Intel or AMD processors.

The app isn't code-signed yet, so if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

It installs for your Windows account (no administrator password needed) and opens. Next time, open **3D Model Configurator** from the Start menu or the desktop.

After that, the app keeps itself up to date. When a new version is out, it downloads it in the background and installs it the next time you open the app (or right away with **Restart now**). Saved builds are in `Documents\3D Model Configurator`, and updates don't touch them.

If you can't run installers, download [`ModelConfigurator-win-x64-Portable.zip`](https://github.com/SpeakingInBits/3d-models/releases/download/v{{VERSION}}/ModelConfigurator-win-x64-Portable.zip) instead, right-click it, choose **Extract All**, and double-click `3D Model Configurator.exe` in the extracted folder.

The other files in **Assets** (`.nupkg`, `.json`) are what installed apps download to update. You don't need them.
